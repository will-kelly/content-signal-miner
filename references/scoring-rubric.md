# Scoring rubric

Score each angle 1–5 on four dimensions. Total out of 20. Show the scores in a table so the ranking can be argued with.

## Evidence (weight: the gate)

How well does the corpus back this angle?

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Validated theme, triangulated across customer voice and sales/support, intense language |
| 4 | Validated theme, two source types, moderate intensity |
| 3 | Meets the validation bar barely; or strong but single-type |
| 2 | Unvalidated lead; recurs but can't be triangulated |
| 1 | Single signal or inference only |

An angle scoring 1 or 2 on evidence cannot be "write now" regardless of total. It goes to hold or kill.

## Whitespace

How much of this ground do competitors already own?

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Customers raise it; no competitor material in the corpus touches it |
| 4 | Competitors mention it in passing; nobody has made it a thesis |
| 3 | Competitors cover it with the standard advice, and customer signals show the standard advice isn't working |
| 2 | Competitors cover it adequately; you'd need a sharper angle to justify it |
| 1 | A competitor has already written the definitive piece and customers cite it |

## Fit

Does the brand have standing to say this?

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Directly in the product's problem space; the brand has first-hand proof |
| 4 | Adjacent; the brand has credible opinion and some proof |
| 3 | Relevant to the audience; brand is a commentator, not an authority |
| 2 | Stretch; would read as opportunistic |
| 1 | Off-strategy or risks contradicting the product's own positioning |

If fit context wasn't supplied, score fit at 3 for everything and say the user needs to adjust it.

## Timing

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Emerging and intensifying; being early is the whole value |
| 4 | Emerging, moderate intensity |
| 3 | Persistent; evergreen, no urgency either way |
| 2 | Persistent but the conversation has calcified; hard to add anything |
| 1 | Fading |

## Verdict rules

- **Write now**: evidence ≥ 3, total ≥ 14, and nothing in the corpus contradicts the thesis
- **Hold**: evidence is the only weak dimension and one named source type would fix it
- **Kill**: evidence ≤ 2 with no obvious path to validate, or whitespace ≤ 2 with no sharper angle available, or fit ≤ 2

Every kill gets one sentence of reason. Every hold names what to collect.
